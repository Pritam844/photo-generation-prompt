POST 9 – AI PHOTO PROMPT

Using a person's photograph as an identification reference, create a high-end cinematic fashion-editorial portrait shot from behind vertically ribbed/reeded textured glass that intensely refracts the face and gives a "scanning" effect. 1:1 (or tight vertical 4:5) framing. Clean side profile, face camera-facing right, head straight, calm and neutral expression (no smile), shoulders relaxed. Clothing: minimal dark top/jacket, no logo, no jewelry. Scene/background: smooth gradient background (#5a0012 → #b10f1c → #ff2a2a) with no visible environment, transitioning from deep burgundy to bright red, with a slight bloom effect. Lighting: Strong and harsh red main/backlight (~2800–3200k, with red gel) from camera-right/rear-right should create bright specular lines on the glass and rim light highlights on the nose/lip/cheek line. Very low neutral fill light from camera-left should maintain an almost silhouette effect. Bright reflections and bands of light should be emphasized along the corrugated glass; high contrast and crushed shadows should be preferred. Optics/Depth of Field: 85mm view, f/2–f/2.8, sharpness should be in the glass plane; corrugated lines should be razor-sharp, while the face is slightly softened due to refraction. Micro-contrast, slight grain, slight halo in bright areas, ultra-clean editorial retouching. Atmosphere: Intense, hypnotic, abstract, modern noir
